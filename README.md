# SonderRotate

A quick settings tile that switches the screen between portrait and landscape without turning your phone.

Tap the tile to swap. Nothing else to it.

- **One permission.** Rotation is a system setting, so the app asks once for *Modify system settings*. No accessibility service, no root.
- **Optional override.** Apps that lock their own orientation ignore the tile. Turning on *Force apps that lock rotation* overrides them, and that part needs [Shizuku](https://shizuku.rikka.app/). Off by default.
- **Material You.** On Android 12 and up the colours come from your wallpaper.
